## FAQ
Q: Where can I find HatSeas's best travel deals?
A: Visit our website's deals section, updated regularly with promotions and discounts.

Q: Can HatSeas help me plan a customized trip?
A: Yes, we offer personalized trip planning. Contact our customer service team.

Q: What documents do I need to travel internationally with HatSeas?
A: Most international travel requires a valid passport and potentially a visa,
depending on destination and citizenship.

Q: What should I pack for Nassau?
A: Lightweight clothing, swimwear, sunscreen, sunglasses, comfortable walking shoes, and
a light jacket for evenings.

Q: Is tipping customary in Nassau?
A: Yes. A 15% gratuity is often included at resorts and restaurants; otherwise tip
15-20% for good service.

Q: How do I get around Nassau?
A: Taxis, ride services, rental cars, local buses (jitneys), and resort shuttles.

Q: What are the requirements for driving?
A: A valid driver's license is required; minimum age depends on the vehicle and our
insurance provider's guidelines (generally 25+ for rentals).

Q: How safe are these tours?
A: Each tour has two guides (front lead and rear escort), a safety tutorial, vehicle
orientation and hand-signal briefing before departure. Guides carry First-Aid kits and
fire extinguishers. Helmets are mandatory for ATV drivers.

Q: Can you accommodate a large group?
A: Up to 50 guests per tour, 100 guests per day across morning and afternoon tours.
Early check-in required; large groups may extend duration by 1-1.5 hours.

Q: Do you offer private tours?
A: Yes, private tours require a minimum of 10 participants. Fewer than 10 incurs an
additional $30.00 per person.
